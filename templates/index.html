Questions, answered

Do I need an account?

No. Open the builder, fill in your details and generate. There's nothing to sign up for.

What happens to my information?

Your details are sent to OpenAI to write your documents and are not stored by ForgeMyCV. A draft of the form is saved in your own browser for up to 7 days so you don't lose your work - use "Clear form" in the builder to remove it.

Will it make things up to impress employers?

No. It's instructed to use only the facts you provide. AI can still make mistakes, so always read your documents through before sending them.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. Go back to your details and generate again, ask for another version, or copy the text as Markdown and finish it in any editor.

How do I get a PDF?

Click "Download PDF" and choose "Save as PDF" in your browser's print dialog. The page is formatted so only your document is printed.
